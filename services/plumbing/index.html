Plumbing jobs around the house

The everyday jobs. A tap that drips, a toilet that won't stop running, a basin you want changed, an outside tap for the garden. These are the jobs I do every week, not the ones I fit in around something bigger.

Call 07846 729203TextGet a quote

What I get called for

  • Toilet changes and toilets that keep running or won't flush properly
  • Basin and tap changes, kitchen and bathroom
  • Leaking, dripping and stiff taps
  • New outside taps
  • Leaks under sinks, behind toilets and on pipework you can get to
  • Isolation valves that won't turn off, or are missing

What happens when I turn up

  1. I'll ask you to send a photo or two first if you can. It saves a wasted trip and means I bring the right bits.
  2. I look at the job, tell you what's wrong and what I'd do, and give you a price before I start.
  3. If you've already bought the tap or toilet, that's fine. I'll check it fits your pipework before I start.
  4. When it's done I test it, clean up, and show you where the isolation valve is.

Questions

Will you come out for a small job like a dripping tap?

Yes. Small jobs are the main part of what I do, not a favour.

Can you fit a tap or toilet I've bought myself?

Usually, yes. Send me a photo of it and of what's there now, and I'll tell you if it'll fit or if it needs extra parts.

Do you give a price before you start?

Yes. You get a price for the job before I pick a tool up.

Do you do bathroom fit-outs?

I don't do full bathroom refits. I do the plumbing jobs in a bathroom: toilets, basins, taps and leaks.

Do you work on boilers?

I don't do boiler or gas work. If it's the boiler, you need a Gas Safe engineer.
