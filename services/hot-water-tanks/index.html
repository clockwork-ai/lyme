Hot water tanks and cylinders, unvented and standard

If your hot water comes from a tank, usually in the airing cupboard, that's a hot water cylinder and I can look after it. I install, service and repair unvented tanks, the mains pressure ones, and standard vented tanks fed from a cold tank in the loft. I'm G3 qualified, which is the qualification you need to work on unvented cylinders.

What I get called for

  • New unvented hot water tanks, and swapping an old one out
  • Yearly servicing of unvented cylinders: the expansion vessel, the pressure reducing valve and the safety valves
  • A dripping tundish, or water coming out of the discharge pipe
  • Standard hot water tanks replaced, or swapped for an unvented one
  • Immersion heater replacement, and tank thermostats
  • Leaking tanks, valves and pipework
  • Hot water cylinders for heat pump systems

What happens when I turn up

  1. Tell me what you've got, a photo of the tank and its label helps, and what's wrong: no hot water, poor pressure, water from a pipe, or it's just old.
  2. I check the tank and everything around it, and tell you if it can be fixed or needs replacing.
  3. You get a price before I do the work.
  4. Fitting an unvented cylinder is notifiable building work. I'll tell you how that's handled on your job before I start.

Questions

Is a hot water tank the same as a cylinder?

Yes. The hot water tank in your airing cupboard is what we call a cylinder. An unvented one runs off the mains, so you get mains pressure at the taps and no tank in the loft. A standard vented one is fed from a cold water tank in the loft, so the pressure is lower.

I've got no hot water. Can you help?

If your hot water comes from a tank, often yes. It's usually the immersion heater, the tank thermostat, a motorised valve or the controls. If you have a combi boiler and no tank, that's a job for a Gas Safe engineer.

Does an unvented hot water tank need servicing?

Yes. It should be checked every year by someone G3 qualified. The safety valves and expansion vessel are there for a reason, and the manufacturer's warranty usually needs it.

Water is coming out of a pipe near my hot water tank. What is it?

Usually the tundish or the discharge pipe. It means one of the safety valves is letting go, often because the expansion vessel has lost its charge. Ring me. If the water coming out is very hot, switch the heating and immersion off first.

Can you swap my old hot water tank for an unvented one?

Often, yes. It depends on your mains pressure and flow, which I'll check first. If your water supply can't feed one properly, I'll tell you.

Are you qualified for unvented hot water tanks?

Yes. I'm G3 qualified, so I can install, service and repair them.
