Smart heating controls

Run your heating from your phone.

I fit smart thermostats and smart radiator valves, wire them in, and set them up with you before I go.

  • Heat the rooms you use, when you use them
  • Change it from the app, wherever you are
  • Swap an old dial or programmer for a smart one
  • Wired in properly, including at the boiler. Nothing on the gas side

Not sure it'll work with your system? Send me a photo of your boiler and the controls on the wall, and I'll tell you before you buy anything.

Heat pumps and heating design

Heat pump not doing its job?

A lot of heat pumps are set up like a boiler, or fitted to radiators and pipes that were never sized for one. I put that right on the water side. I'm not selling new ones.

  • Flow temperature and weather compensation set up properly
  • Cold rooms, short cycling and high running costs looked into
  • Radiators and pipework checked against what the house needs
  • Controls set up for a heat pump, not a boiler

Heating is my background, so I do heating design too: room-by-room heat loss, radiator sizing and full system layouts. For homeowners, and for other installers.
