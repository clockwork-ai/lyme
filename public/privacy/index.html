Privacy

What happens to the details you send me, including calls that may be answered and recorded.

Who I am

Callum Broadfoot, a sole trader trading as Lyme Plumbing & Heating, based in High Lane, Stockport SK6. I decide what happens to your details. You can contact me on 07846 729203, on WhatsApp, or callum@lymeplumbing.co.uk.

What I collect

If you use the quote form, I get what you type in: your name, phone number, email if you give it, postcode, the job, your message, any photos, how soon you need someone, and the best time to call. The form asks you to tick that I can contact you about the job.

If you ring or text 07846 729203, I get your number and what you tell me. If you use WhatsApp, I get your number and the message. If you sign up for the monthly guide, I get your email and your first name if you give it.

If you use Ask Lyme, I get the messages you type. If you pay by card, Stripe takes the payment. I keep the invoice. This website does not store your full card number.

The site counts page visits with Plausible. That count does not use cookies and it does not give me your name. It can record that someone clicked Call, Text, Email or WhatsApp, or sent a quote, and which page they were on.

AI call answering and recording

Calls to 07846 729203 may be answered by an AI receptionist called Jess, when I cannot pick up. Jess uses ElevenLabs for the voice. The call is handled through Twilio. Texts to that number come through Twilio as well. WhatsApp is a separate number.

A call may be recorded, and a written transcript may be made, so I can follow up the enquiry. From a call I may hold your phone number, the date and time, the recording, the transcript, and anything you say, such as your name, your postcode and the job.

I use the recording and the transcript to answer the call, to ring or message you back, and to quote or do the job if you ask me to. I do not sell them.

If you want a person, say so on the call. You can also hang up and text 07846 729203, use WhatsApp, or email callum@lymeplumbing.co.uk, and I will deal with it myself. If you do not want the call recorded, say that at the start of the call. You can also ask me afterwards to delete a recording or a transcript, and I will.

Why, and the lawful basis

I use your details to reply, to quote, to do the job, to take payment, to keep my accounts, and to send the monthly guide if you asked for it. I don't sell your details.

  • Quote and job enquiries, including the form, the chat, calls, texts and WhatsApp: to take the steps you asked for before a contract, and to carry out the contract if I do the work.
  • Invoices and other financial records: because the law says I have to keep them.
  • The monthly guide: because you consented. You can withdraw that consent any time. Withdrawing it does not affect anything already done with your consent.
  • Call recordings and transcripts: my legitimate interest in following up a call you made. You can object, and you can ask me not to record the call.
  • Page visit counts: my legitimate interest in seeing which pages are used, without cookies and without your name.
  • Card payments: to carry out the contract. Stripe takes the payment.

Who else handles your details

These companies process details for me.

  • Twilio (United States). Calls and texts to 07846 729203 go through Twilio, including calls answered by Jess.
  • ElevenLabs (United States). The voice for Jess. A call may be recorded and transcribed through ElevenLabs.
  • Stripe (United States). Card payments. This website does not take your card number. Stripe does, when you pay by card.
  • Cloudflare (United States). Hosts this website. Sends the quote form and the guide signup to my email. Runs Ask Lyme with Cloudflare Workers AI, so the messages you type are processed to write a reply.
  • Google (United States). My business email is a Google inbox, which is where quote and signup emails arrive. Google also holds my Google Business Profile, including reviews people choose to leave there. This site does not load fonts from Google.
  • Plausible (European Union). Cookieless page counts, from the script on this site. It does not give me a list of visitors by name.
  • Clockwork Jobs (hosted on Vercel in the United States). When you send the quote form, a copy of your name, phone, email, postcode, the job, your message, how soon you need someone, and the best time to call is sent there so I can pick the enquiry up. Photos stay in the email. They are not sent to Clockwork Jobs.
  • Meta (United States). WhatsApp messages are handled by WhatsApp, which Meta runs. The site links you there. WhatsApp is not the calls and texts number.

International transfers

Twilio, ElevenLabs, Stripe, Cloudflare, Google and Meta are based in the United States. Clockwork Jobs is hosted on Vercel, which is based in the United States. If one of them handles your details, those details may be processed in the United States. Plausible is based in the European Union.

How long I keep it

Invoices and other financial records are kept for 6 years.

A quote enquiry that does not become a job, including the form, photos, texts, WhatsApp messages and notes from a call, is kept for 12 months from the last contact, then deleted.

Photos and messages about a job I did are kept for 6 years with the invoice.

A call recording and transcript are kept for 12 months, or sooner if you ask me to delete them. A short note I need for the invoice stays with the financial record for 6 years. The recording itself does not.

A monthly guide signup is kept until you ask me to stop. I also clear signup emails that are more than 2 years old if I have not heard from you.

Ask Lyme messages are used to write the reply. This site does not keep its own copy of the chat after that.

Plausible does not give me a named list of visitors, so I do not keep one.

Cookies

This site doesn't set cookies, and it doesn't load fonts from Google. The type is stored with the site. Plausible counts visits without cookies.

The chat

If you use Ask Lyme, the messages you type are sent to this site so a reply can be written. The reply is generated with Cloudflare Workers AI. I use those messages only to answer you. This site does not keep its own copy of the chat after the reply. If you send the quote form from the chat, it emails me in the same way as the form on the contact page, and a copy of the enquiry (without photos) goes to Clockwork Jobs.

The quote form

Sending the form emails me what you typed, plus any photos you attached. The email goes through Cloudflare to my Google inbox. A copy of the words, without the photos, also goes to Clockwork Jobs. I use that to reply, quote and do the job.

The monthly guide

If you sign up on the guides page, I get your email address and your first name if you give it. I use that to send one short guide a month. I don't use it for anything else, and you can withdraw that consent any time. Signing up emails me your details. It does not email you a guide.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask me for:

  • access, which means telling you what I hold about you
  • rectification, which means correcting it if it is wrong
  • erasure, which means deleting it
  • restriction, which means limiting how I use it
  • objection, which means stopping a use that relies on legitimate interests, including call recording and page counts
  • portability, which means a copy in a common format, where the use is a contract or your consent
  • not to be subject to a decision made only by automated means that has a legal or similarly significant effect on you. I don't make decisions like that. Jess takes the details of a call, and I decide on any quote or job myself.

You can withdraw consent where I rely on it. The monthly guide is the main place I use consent. Asking me not to record a call, or to delete a recording, is covered in the section above.

Ring, text or WhatsApp me, or email callum@lymeplumbing.co.uk. If you're not happy with how I've handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
