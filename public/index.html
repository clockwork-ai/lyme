Call-out rates

A call-out is a short-notice visit to find a fault and fix it. I supply all the materials. Materials are extra.

  • Booked weekday or Saturday morning£60 for the first hour. No call-out fee. Larger jobs are a free quote. Payment on completion. Card payments accepted.
  • Same-day or urgent£90, including the first hour, then £60 an hour. The first hour is paid before I set off.
  • Evening, weekend, bank holiday and overnight emergencies£135, including the first hour, then £90 an hour. Evenings are 6pm to 10pm. Overnight is 10pm to 7am. Saturday after 1pm counts as the weekend. Sunday emergencies are 8am to 10pm only, at this rate. The first hour is paid before I set off.
