Houses in New Mills, and heat pumps

New Mills is SK22, in the High Peak. A lot of the housing is stone terraces built for the mills, climbing the hills, plus cottages and farms out towards Rowarth, Thornsett and Hayfield.

In a stone terrace the radiators and the pipework decide a heat pump, not the view. Rooms can be small, the walls are thick, and the cylinder is often in a tight cupboard. I look at all of that before I say if a pump is the right job.

Out of the town, a lot of the farms and older cottages are off the gas. Oil, LPG or a solid-fuel stove is common out there. An air source heat pump can suit a house like that, on a wet system, once the radiators or the underfloor heating will actually heat it.

A new heat pump in New Mills

An air source heat pump sits outside and feeds a wet heating system: radiators, underfloor heating, or both, and a hot water cylinder. Before I say if it's the right job, I check:

  • Where the outdoor unit can go, with air around it and a solid base, and how the pipes get inside
  • Whether the radiators or the underfloor can heat the rooms at a lower temperature
  • The cylinder, the valves and the controls
  • How you will run it day to day, so it is set up before I leave

I've fitted Mitsubishi Ecodan, Grant Aerona and Vaillant aroTHERM heat pumps. More on the heat pump installation page.

Heat pump servicing and repair in New Mills

If the pump is already in, I can come and look, whoever fitted it. An annual service covers the filters and strainers, the system water and pressure, the flow, the cylinder and the controls, and what the outdoor unit is doing on a normal run, including defrost. I tell you what I found, in plain words, before I suggest any work.

Fault-finding is the part I like. Tell me what it's doing:

  • Not heating the house, or some rooms staying cold
  • Running costs that have jumped, with no change in how you use it
  • New noises, error codes, or defrost that won't finish
  • Low flow or pressure, or hot water that isn't hot enough

You get a price before I do any repair. The rates are on the heat pump servicing and repair page.

What I don't do

I don't do boiler or gas work, and I don't work on an oil boiler. If a gas boiler has to come off, you'll need a gas engineer for that part. I don't do refrigerant work either. If the fault is in the refrigerant circuit, I'll say so and stop there.
